*All submitted applications will be considered for mentorship

please let us know via any of our communication channels.*

## Application Submission Workflow
Applicants are highly encouraged to attend the info session on 27th of August at 5:30PM Nepali time
over [meet.google.com/ekx-ksgx-inf](meet.google.com/ekx-ksgx-inf). And if you would like to add it to your 
[google calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/event?action=TEMPLATE&tmeid=NG1sZ2E1MHR0aWZyNmtrN2djdXVlbjl1bmwgbnl1LmVkdV9xams3YmdoMHBlMjNrMWRlNW9tYm83YWJoNEBn&tmsrc=nyu.edu_qjk7bgh0pe23k1de5ombo7abh4%40group.calendar.google.com)

Application must be submitted via email to [hello@gajabaar.io](mailto:hello@gajabaar.io)
with subject "Gajabaar 2026 application".
In your application, include:
1. a paragraph about yourself.
2. a paragraph about what you are interested in (cybersecurity or otherwise).
3. a sample of your work. This could be your school notes (can be paper based or digital but must be in pdf format),
or a blog/vlog you maintain, or code/writeup you have written or anything else that is your own output.

You will be invited to an online interview after your application. In the interview, 
you should be prepared to discuss the content of your application. You should also be 
prepared to answer questions about your interest in our program.
